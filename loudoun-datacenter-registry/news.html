In the news

Reporting on data centers and AI infrastructure in Loudoun County and across Virginia, collected automatically from public news feeds.

These are headlines and links, nothing more. Inclusion is not endorsement, and this project has no relationship with any outlet listed. Every link opens on the publisher's own site — please read them there, because local reporting on this subject only continues if people read it.

—

Where these come from

Headlines are collected from Google News searches and from the feeds published by Virginia Mercury, Cardinal News and WTOP, filtered to stories mentioning data centers, hyperscale development or AI infrastructure.

The collection is automated and unreviewed. Automated feeds occasionally surface press releases and search-optimised filler dressed as news, and a headline appearing here is not a judgement that the story is accurate or that this project agrees with it.

Only the headline, publication, date and link are stored — no article text, and no AI-written summary. Summarising an article nobody here has read would be inventing, and a link is what the reporting deserves anyway.

Missing an outlet that covers this well? Tell us and we will add its feed.
