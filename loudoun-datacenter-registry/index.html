Loudoun County, Virginia

The data centers are mapped. The people living beside them are not.

Loudoun County holds the largest concentration of data centers on earth. The county publishes where every one of them is. Nobody publishes what it is like to live next to one — those accounts are scattered across board meetings, neighbourhood groups and local news. This map puts both on the same screen.
